# ADR-008: Pricing and idempotent preparation gate

Status: TRANSITIONAL. Approval basis: current user's explicit architecture and contract-freeze execution authorization, 2026-09-11; not a claim of historical or production approval.

## Decision
Isolated Woo+WoodMart+B2BKing whole-cart runtime quote, exact parity matrix, revalidation and concurrent stock proof before checkout. Durable unique transaction mapping, not metadata alone.

## Rationale
Protect both online and POS commercial truth.

## Supersedes
Raw product prices as checkout authority; assumed reservation behavior.

## Consequences
Consumers update through the shared contract process. Preserve historical references and provider mappings. All unverified live facts stay UNVERIFIED until CP-04/gate evidence. This ADR does not authorize untested production effects.

## Affected contracts
Quote, PreparedSale, SaleResolution.

## Affected workstreams
WS1, WS2, WS3; implementation ownership stays in OWNERSHIP.md.

## Provenance
S0 current master prompt; reconciled S1/S2/S3 and targeted S4 where applicable. See docs/source-manifest/DECISION-PROVENANCE.md.
